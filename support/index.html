Help with Swarm Street

Write to us: contact@swipelijk.com

It helps if you tell us your device, what you were doing, and what happened. A screenshot is welcome. Please do not send passwords or payment details; the game never needs them.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device only. There is no account and nothing is stored online by the game. If you delete the game, its progress is deleted with it.

How do I start again?

Open SETTINGS on the title screen and choose NEW GAME.

How do I change the language?

Open SETTINGS and choose LANGUAGE. The game starts in your device's language when it is one of the languages it has.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open SETTINGS and use NOTES. You can also switch them off in your device's own notification settings.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works offline and does not contact any server.

Are there adverts or purchases?

Not in this version. If that changes, the privacy policy will be updated before the new version is released.

I found a bug or a wrong translation

Please email us with the night you were on and, if you can, a screenshot. Translation corrections from native speakers are very welcome.
