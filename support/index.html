Playing

Where is my progress kept?

On your phone only. There is no account, and the game stores nothing online. If you delete the game, its progress is deleted with it. A phone backup (for example iCloud) may bring it back.

How do I start again?

Open SETTINGS on the title screen and choose NEW GAME. Your old progress is set aside on the phone.

How do I change the language?

Open SETTINGS and choose LANGUAGE. The game starts in your phone's language when it is one of the languages it has.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open SETTINGS and use NOTES. You can also switch them off in your phone's own notification settings.

Does the game need the internet?

Not to play. It uses the internet for three things only:

  1. when the game starts, and when you come back to it, it asks Apple's App Store for the Supporter Pack's price and whether you own it;
  2. once you have chosen to watch an advert, Google's advert software loads adverts;
  3. just before adverts start, the game fetches one small "off-switch" file from this website.

Without a connection you can still play. You just get no adverts, and the pack cannot be bought until you are online.

Adverts

Do I have to watch adverts?

Bonus adverts are always your choice: the game offers a bonus if you watch one, and every night can be won without them. From night 6 the game may also show a short advert between nights (at most one every three nights and four a day), once you have chosen to watch a bonus advert at least once. The Supporter Pack removes those.

How do I switch advert offers off?

Open SETTINGS and switch ADVERT OFFERS off. Switching them back on asks for the grown-ups' check (a sum to answer).

How do I change my answer to Google's question?

In Europe: open SETTINGS and choose PRIVACY CHOICES. It appears once you have been asked, while ADVERT OFFERS is on. The privacy policy explains what Google receives.

Why is there no advert button right now?

There are none during the first two nights, and there is a daily limit. Sometimes Google has no advert to show; if you had already tapped, the game usually gives you the bonus anyway.

I saw an advert that was wrong, rude or not for my age

Please tell us. Open SETTINGS and choose REPORT AN ADVERT. It lists the last five adverts shown (where in the game, the day and the time). Tap COPY and email it to us, and say what the advert was for and what was wrong with it. A screenshot helps most. The number on the list is the game's own counter, not Google's; it helps us find the moment so the advert can be reported to Google.

The Supporter Pack

What is it?

One optional purchase. It gives all the cannon looks at once, it gives the advert bonuses without the advert, and it removes the adverts between nights. Every look can also be earned by playing. You never need the pack to finish the game.

What does it cost, and who sells it?

The price is shown by the App Store before you buy, in your own currency, with tax included where the law says so. You pay once; it is not a subscription. You buy it through Apple's App Store: Apple takes the payment and sends the receipt. The pack itself is made by us, and we are responsible for it working as described.

I am under 18

Ask a parent or guardian before you buy. If your family uses Ask to Buy, the game shows "WAITING FOR A GROWN-UP TO SAY YES" until they answer.

I bought it but I do not have it (or I have a new phone)

Open SETTINGS, choose SUPPORTER PACK, then RESTORE PURCHASE. Use the same Apple account you bought it with. Family Sharing is on, so family members in your Apple family group can restore it too. If the pack's price does not load, the card shows TRY AGAIN.

Can I cancel or get my money back?

Refunds are handled by Apple, because Apple took the payment. Go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com , sign in, choose "Request a refund" and pick the Supporter Pack. Apple usually answers within two days.

If you live in the European Union, the EEA or the United Kingdom, the law gives you 14 days to cancel most online purchases. For a digital item this right can end once the item has been delivered to you at your request and you agreed to that when buying. Apple's terms explain how Apple applies this, and Apple decides the request. Nothing on this page takes away rights the law gives you.

If a refund is given, the game takes back the looks the pack gave. Looks you earned by playing stay.

The pack does not work

Write to us. If something you paid for does not work as described, you have a legal right to have it put right, and we will fix it. If we cannot fix it in a reasonable time, you can ask for your money back through Apple.
