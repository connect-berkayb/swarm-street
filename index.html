Tap and hold

Tap for quick shots, hold to charge a blast, and let go at the right moment for a perfect hit.

Build the line

Spend what the robots drop on turrets and barricades between surges, then upgrade what works.

Light the city

Thirty-five nights across seven districts, from the market to the rail yard. Every street you hold lights up on the map.
