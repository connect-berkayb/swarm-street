Swarm Street: Privacy Policy

In force from: the release of the first version of Swarm Street with adverts and the Supporter Pack. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What changed: This page replaces the earlier one, which described a game with no adverts, nothing to buy and no internet use. The game now has video adverts (through Google) and one optional purchase (through Apple's App Store). This page explains both. The game does not ask your age.

The short version

  • You can play the whole game without adverts and without paying.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We, the developer, have no server. We never receive your name, your age or your progress.
  • There are two kinds of advert. Bonus adverts are always your choice: you tap to watch one for a bonus. From night 6 the game may also show a short advert between nights. Both come through advert software made by Google, which receives some information about your phone (listed below).
  • Adverts are never personalised. The game never asks for your phone's advertising ID and never asks to track you across other apps.
  • In Europe, adverts appear only after you say yes to Google's question.
  • There is one optional purchase, the Supporter Pack. It removes the adverts between nights. You buy it through Apple. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

The game saves these things in files on your own phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else by the game:

  • your progress (nights won, stars, bolts, workshop upgrades) and your settings, including your language;
  • the days you played, so the game can pace its gifts;
  • whether you own the Supporter Pack;
  • how many advert bonuses you used today, and a short list of the last five adverts shown (place, day and time), so you can report a bad advert;
  • your answer about adverts, if you were asked;
  • the last answer of the "off-switch" check described below.

NEW GAME in SETTINGS starts again from night 1. It sets your old progress aside on the phone; it does not erase your advert answer. Deleting the game deletes all of it. If your phone makes backups (for example to iCloud), a backup may contain these files and may bring them back when you restore the phone. Those backups are run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Reminders

The game can show reminders such as "Your post has arrived." They are off unless you turn them on. You can turn them off again in SETTINGS (NOTES) or in your phone's own notification settings. Reminders are planned on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else to show them.

Adverts

Bonus adverts are always your choice. Sometimes the game offers a bonus (for example double the bolts you just won) if you watch a video advert. You never have to: every night can be won without one. Bonus adverts are offered from night 3.

Adverts between nights. From night 6 the game may show a short full-screen advert after a night you won: at most one every three nights and four a day, never on a night where you watched a bonus advert, and only once you have chosen to watch a bonus advert at least once. Owners of the Supporter Pack never see them.

What happens when you tap an advert offer

  1. Tapping WATCH AD is your choice to see the advert. A small card shows while it loads; CANCEL stops it.
  2. The game lets Google's software ask Google whether Google's consent question is needed where you are. If it is, Google's question appears. This step already contacts Google: before you have answered, Google receives information such as your phone's internet address (IP address), which app is asking and details of your phone.
  3. The game fetches a small "off-switch" file from our website (see below).
  4. Only if every step allows it does Google's advert software start and load adverts.

After you have said yes once, the game repeats steps 2 to 4 by itself when you open the game, so that adverts are ready. It does not ask you again each time.

Until you tap an advert offer, the game does not start Google's software.

What Google receives

Once it runs, Google's advert software (Google AdMob, with Google's consent tool) receives:

  • your phone's internet address, from which Google can tell roughly where you are (such as your country or city);
  • details of your phone, such as its model and system version;
  • an identifier that Google's software creates for this game on your phone;
  • which adverts you were shown, whether you watched them to the end and whether you tapped them;
  • measurements of how Google's own software is working (crashes, speed, battery use).

Google uses this to choose an advert that fits the moment (the game, your rough location, your kind of phone), to show it, to limit how often you see the same one, to count views, to stop fraud and to fix its software. Google may pass information to the advertising companies it works with; in Europe, Google's question lists them.

What we ask Google for

For every player, of any age, the game asks Google only for adverts that are:

  • not personalised (not chosen from a profile of you);
  • handled the way Google handles adverts for teenagers;
  • rated for teenagers ("T") or milder.

The game never asks for your phone's advertising ID and never shows Apple's "allow tracking" question, so on an iPhone that ID is not available to Google's software.

Google's role

The company is Google Ireland Limited for players in Europe. Google decides for itself how it uses what its software receives, under its own rules. We chose to put Google's software in the game, so we are responsible for that choice and for asking you first. We never receive this information ourselves and have no copy of it.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://business.safety.google/privacy/
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google may handle this information on computers outside Europe, including in the United States. Google explains on the pages above how it protects information that leaves Europe.

Saying no, or changing your mind

  • Simply never tap an advert offer, or press CANCEL while one loads.
  • Switch ADVERT OFFERS off in SETTINGS at any time. (Switching it back on asks for the grown-ups' check.)
  • In Europe, PRIVACY CHOICES in SETTINGS opens Google's question again so you can change your answer. It appears once you have been asked, while ADVERT OFFERS is on. Opening it contacts Google again.
  • If you own the Supporter Pack, Google's advert software is never started.

After a no, the game asks Google for no more adverts. To be sure Google's software has fully stopped, close the game and open it again. Saying no does not undo what happened before, and it does not delete what Google already received; for that, use Google's pages above.

If you are in Europe (the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland)

Google's question. Before the first advert, Google's question appears. Google's form may ask about more things than this game uses (for example personalised adverts). Whatever you answer there, this game only ever asks Google for adverts that are not personalised. If you do not allow Google to store or read information on your phone, the game shows no adverts and remembers your no on your phone. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you answer.

How the game decides you are in Europe. It looks at your phone's region setting and the country of your App Store account. Google's software also makes its own check of where you are.

The Supporter Pack

The Supporter Pack is one optional purchase. It gives all the cannon looks at once, it gives the advert bonuses without the advert, and it removes the adverts between nights. Every look can also be earned by playing.

You buy it through Apple's App Store. Apple takes the payment and sends the receipt. We never see your name, your Apple account or your payment details. Each time the game starts, and when you come back to it, for every player, it asks the App Store on your phone three things: the pack's price, whether this Apple account owns the pack (also through Family Sharing), and the country of the App Store account. The game keeps "owned: yes or no" on your phone. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Apple handles the purchase under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ . Refunds and cancelling are explained on our support page.

The off-switch file, and this website

Just before the advert software starts, the game fetches one small file from our website. The file only says whether adverts are currently switched on, so we can switch them off for everyone if something goes wrong. The game fetches it at most once a day. The request carries no identifier and no information about you.

As with any web page, the company that hosts our website sees the internet address of whoever fetches a page or that file, and keeps it in its logs for a time. Our host is GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This website itself uses no cookies, no adverts and no visitor counting.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write (and the advert list, if you paste it). We use it only to answer you and to fix the game. We do not add you to any mailing list. Our email provider stores the messages for us. We delete a conversation 12 months after its last message, or sooner if you ask.

If you test an early version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shows us the email address you joined with and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to fix the game.

Children and teenagers

Swarm Street is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not made for children under 13.

  • The game does not ask anyone's age. Every player gets the same kind of advert: never personalised, handled the way Google handles adverts for teenagers, and rated for teenagers or milder.
  • In Europe, adverts appear only after a yes to Google's question. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you answer it.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We have no accounts and no server, so we hold nothing about any player.
  • Parents: you can switch ADVERT OFFERS off in SETTINGS; switching it back on needs the grown-ups' check. Your phone's own settings (Screen Time, Ask to Buy) can block purchases. If you think your child's information reached Google through this game, write to us and we will help you with the steps.

Why we are allowed to do this

European law says we must have a reason ("legal ground") for each use of personal information:

WhatOur legal ground
Showing adverts through Google's software, including what it stores and reads on your phoneYour consent. You can take it back at any time (see "Saying no, or changing your mind").
Letting Google's consent tool check whether it must ask you, and remembering your answerNeeded to ask you properly and to prove what you chose (our legitimate interest and a legal duty).
Fetching the off-switch file; keeping our website safeOur legitimate interest in being able to stop adverts quickly and in a working website.
Checking and delivering the Supporter PackNeeded to give you what you bought (a contract).
Answering your emailsOur legitimate interest in helping players, or a contract if it is about a purchase.

You do not have to say yes to anything. If you do not, you get no adverts; the rest of the game is the same. The game makes no decisions about you that have legal or similarly serious effects.

How long things are kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the game (or the phone backup that holds it). The advert list holds only the last five adverts. Your advert answer stays until the game is deleted.
  • With us: nothing from the game. Emails: 12 months after the last message.
  • With Google, Apple and our web host: for the time their own policies say (links above).

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws in the United Kingdom and Switzerland) you can:

  • ask what personal information is held about you and get a copy;
  • have it corrected or deleted, or have its use limited;
  • take back your consent at any time: ADVERT OFFERS and PRIVACY CHOICES in SETTINGS;
  • complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You may also go to the authority in the country where you live.

You can also object. Where we rely on a "legitimate interest" (the off-switch file, the consent check, your emails), you may object at any time by writing to us.

What this means in practice: from the game we hold nothing, and we cannot tell which player you are, so there is nothing we can look up, send or delete for the game itself. You delete what is on your phone by deleting the game. For your emails, write to us and we will answer within one month. For what Google or Apple hold, use their pages above; if that does not work, write to us and we will help.

A parent or guardian can use these rights for a child.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, no email address, no contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No exact location. (Google's software can tell your rough area from your internet address, as described above.)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • No selling of your information.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will change this page before that version is released, change the date at the top and say what changed.
